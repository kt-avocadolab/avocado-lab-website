Privacy Policy
for Flow Squat

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Flow Squat, provided by Avocado Lab, judges squat depth using your device’s front camera. The app does not collect personal data, create user accounts, run analytics, or show advertising.

Camera use

Flow Squat accesses the front camera only after you grant permission, and only to analyse your squat in real time. The camera feed is processed entirely on your device. It is never recorded, stored as video, or uploaded. Camera frames do not leave your phone.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your training sessions—including dates, rep counts and per-rep verdicts—on your device so you can review them in History. This information stays on your device until you delete a session in the app or remove the app.

Because Avocado Lab does not receive this information, there is no server-side account or personal data record for us to delete.

Audio

The app plays short audio cues for the countdown and rep verdicts using sounds bundled inside the app. No audio is recorded or transmitted. Flow Squat does not request microphone access.

Data sharing and tracking

We do not sell or share personal data. No account or sign-in is required. No analytics or advertising SDKs are included, and the app does not track you across apps or websites.

Children’s privacy

Flow Squat does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is released. The effective date at the top of this page will show when the latest version took effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Avocado Lab at info@avocado-lab.com.
