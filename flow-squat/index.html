Start your set.

01

Set down your phone

Place your iPhone on a stable surface with its front camera facing your side or rear position.

02

Let the frame lock

Flow Squat checks that your full movement is visible and automatically recognises the supported placement.

03

Train with feedback

04 Honest scope

A training aid.
Not the final judge.

Flow Squat focuses on squat depth during personal wall-ball practice. It does not assess ball weight, throwing technique or target hits. It is not an official competition product, is not affiliated with HYROX, and does not replace a human judge.
