Privacy Policy
for Training Tempo

Effective date: October 23, 2026

Training Tempo is a workout interval timer provided by Avocado Lab. It works without an account or internet connection. We do not collect personal data through the app.

Information stored on your device

The app saves workouts you add to Library and recent workout settings on your device. It also stores your sound cue and screen awake preferences on your device. This information stays on your device and is not sent to Avocado Lab or a third party. You can delete saved workouts and recent entries in the app; removing the app removes its local data.

Because we do not receive this information, we do not have a server-side account or personal data record to access or delete.

Network access and permissions

Workout timing, audio cues, and local storage work offline. The app does not request camera, microphone, location, or health data access. If you choose to open the Privacy Policy link in Settings, your browser connects to this website under its own privacy settings.

Data sharing and tracking

We do not sell or share data from the app. The app does not include analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children's privacy

Training Tempo does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy before the change is released. The effective date above shows when this version took effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Avocado Lab at info@avocado-lab.com.
