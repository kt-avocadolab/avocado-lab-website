01 Made for coaching

Less clock-watching.
More coaching.

In a group session, the timer is part of the room. Training Beat is being designed to help coaches prepare a structured interval workout and make its current phase easy to read from a distance.

It is a practical class timer—not a training plan, performance tracker or connected fitness platform.

02 Planned for v1.0

Set the structure.
Stay with the session.

01 / BUILD

Shape each block

Set up a workout with multiple Blocks, repeated Sets and work intervals. Split work into continuous Segments when your format calls for it.

02 / COACH

Make the phase clear

A large countdown is designed to distinguish work from rest at a glance, with straightforward controls for pausing, resuming or moving on.

03 / RETURN

Reuse your setup

Save workout templates in your Library and revisit recent session settings on the same device.

These describe the current v1.0 product plan. Features and final behavior may change during development and testing.

04 Private by design

Your workouts
stay with you.

Training Beat is designed to work offline and keep workout settings on your device.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No advertising or analytics services
  • Workout settings and recent records stored locally
  • No camera, microphone or location access for the core timer
